Raporty OSP — Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026. The Polish version (Polityka prywatności) is the primary text.

In short: the app has no server, no ads and no usage analytics. The developer receives no data. Everything users enter is stored on their phone and — if their fire brigade unit uses synchronisation — in the unit's folder on the Google Drive of the account that created the unit.

1. Who is responsible for the app

Raporty OSP (“the app”) is developed and distributed by its author (mionsek), contact: mionskowski.dawid@gmail.com.

The app is a tool for Polish volunteer fire brigades (Ochotnicza Straż Pożarna, OSP). The fire brigade unit using the app is the controller of the personal data entered into it — data of its members and of people named in its documents. The developer has no access to this data and does not process it.

2. What data the app holds

The app stores only what users enter:

  • Unit details — name, town and station address.
  • Firefighters — first and last name, rank, roles (driver, commander, first aid) and medical examination expiry date.
  • Vehicles and equipment — names, designations, registration numbers, fuel consumption rates.
  • Call-outs — date, times, incident address, type of hazard, vehicle crews.
  • Property handovers — place and time, description of the property, and the name, address and phone number of the person who took it over.
  • Vehicle trips and equipment work — odometer readings, routes, purposes, drivers, equipment running time.
  • Entry author — when synchronising, call-outs, handovers and trips record the e-mail address of the Google account that added them and, on a shared unit account, which firefighter entered them.

3. Where data is stored

On the phone

All data is stored in the app's private storage on the device. The app disables Android backup, so its data is not copied to the user's Google device backup.

On the unit's Google Drive (optional)

If a user signs in with Google and creates or joins a unit, the unit's data is stored in an OSP_App_… folder on the Google Drive of the account that created the unit, and shared with people invited by the unit's administrator. When someone is invited, Google sends them an e-mail with the unit name and invitation code. Data on Google Drive is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Access to the Google account

When signing in, the app requests access to Google Drive. This is needed because one person creates the unit's folder and other members add and read documents in it — Google's narrower Drive permission does not let users see files created by their colleagues. The app works only with the unit's folder; it does not read, modify or delete any other files on Drive. From the Google account the app also reads the e-mail address and name, to show who is signed in and to mark the author of entries.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not transferred to the developer or anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Location and Bluetooth

  • Location is used only when the user taps “Wstaw adres z GPS” (insert address from GPS) on a call-out or handover. The app then determines the phone's position once and converts it into an address using the Android system geocoding service (on most phones provided by Google). Only the address is saved — not coordinates or a location history.
  • Bluetooth is used to find, pair and connect to a portable thermal printer. Only the printed document is sent to the printer. On older Android versions (up to 11) the system also requires location permission to scan for Bluetooth devices.

6. Sharing of data

The developer does not collect, sell or share any data. Data leaves the phone only:

  • to the unit's folder on Google Drive — when the unit uses synchronisation;
  • where the user sends it — a printout, PDF or Excel file sent through an app of the user's choice (e.g. e-mail or a messenger) or to a printer;
  • to the geocoding service — only with “Wstaw adres z GPS”, as described above.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Data on the phone is kept until the user deletes it. In the app's Settings, “Wyloguj” → “Wyloguj i usuń dane” (sign out and delete data) removes the unit's data from the phone; uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the phone.
  • Data in the unit's Google Drive folder remains there when one user signs out — its retention and deletion are decided by the unit (the folder owner). An entry deleted in the app disappears from all of the unit's phones; its file in the Drive folder remains with a deletion marker (so that the entry does not come back from another phone) and can be removed permanently directly in the unit's Drive folder.
  • The retention period for documents is determined by the unit in line with the regulations that apply to it.

8. Rights of data subjects

People whose data was entered into the app (unit members, people taking over property) can exercise their rights under the GDPR — including access, rectification and erasure — by contacting the fire brigade unit that entered the data. The developer has no access to this data but is happy to help with questions about how the app works.

9. Security

Connections to Google are encrypted (HTTPS). Only people invited by the unit's administrator can access the unit's folder. Users should protect phones holding unit data with a screen lock.

10. Children

The app is intended for adult members of fire brigade units and is not directed at children.

11. Changes to this policy

Changes are published on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes are also described in the release notes of the app.
